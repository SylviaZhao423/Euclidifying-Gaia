\chapter{Spatial Resolutions of TelePix2}

The spatial resolutions of TelePix2 were also estimated by fitting the spatial residual distributions with Gaussian function. The resolutions were measured to be $63.474 \pm 0.130 \mu m$ in x direction and $37.276 \pm 0.098 \mu m$.
